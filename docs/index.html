C2 Fit

Short, honest recipes for people who want to eat well without cooking becoming a hobby. Every recipe is built around the same promise: fast, cheap, and high in protein, using ingredients from a normal supermarket.

Recipes are published daily as image posts on TikTok (@c2_fit_), with other platforms to follow.

Each post carries:

  • Per-serving calories, protein, carbs, and fat
  • An estimated grocery cost per serving
  • The full ingredient list and numbered steps

Nutrition figures are estimates calculated from the listed ingredients, not laboratory measurements. They are intended as a guide, not medical or dietary advice.
