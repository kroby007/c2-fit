Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

This policy covers the C2 Fit publishing tool, a private automation that posts recipe content to social media accounts owned and operated by us — currently the TikTok account @c2_fit_. The tool is not offered to the public and has no user accounts.

Information we collect

From visitors to this website: none. This site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about the people who visit it.

From social platforms: the tool holds access credentials for the social media accounts it publishes to. These credentials belong to the account owner and authorise publishing only. The tool does not read follower lists, messages, comments, or any other personal information about the people who view or interact with the posts.

How information is used

Stored credentials are used for one purpose: uploading recipe images and captions to the connected accounts. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling, or any form of tracking, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Storage and retention

Access credentials are held as encrypted repository secrets and are readable only by the automation that publishes posts. They are deleted when the corresponding platform integration is removed. Published images are served as static files from this site.

Third-party services

The tool sends content to TikTok in order to publish it, and may be extended to other social platforms in future. Once published, that content is governed by the relevant platform's own privacy policy and terms.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the connected account's settings on the platform in question. Doing so immediately stops all publishing.
